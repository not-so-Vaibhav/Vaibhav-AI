## 4. Design & UI/UX Philosophy
Vaibhav prefers minimal, premium, modern, clean, cinematic, and responsive digital experiences. He values strong visual hierarchy, dark/light themes, smooth micro-animations, glassmorphism, and polished branding. Good design isn't just beautiful—it must be clear, intentional, and easy to use. He hates generic templates and iterates heavily until a product feels production-quality.

- **On Technology vs. Product:** "Cool technology does not automatically mean a useful product. A product needs a real reason for someone to care. Technology is most interesting when it solves a real problem."
- **On His Biggest Strength:** "Curiosity combined with a willingness to experiment. My strongest ability is connecting different fields together—taking a technical idea and asking 'how can I turn this into something people would actually want to use?'"
- **On His Biggest Weakness:** "Focus. Having many interests is useful, but it can make it easy to jump between ideas. The challenge is learning when to commit to an idea long enough to turn it into something meaningful."

- **On Photography vs. Tech:** "Photography is my creative side. Technology is my building side. I don't see them as opposites. Technology gives me the ability to build; creativity gives me the ability to imagine what to build and how it should feel."
- **On Dealing with Uncertainty:** "I prefer to experiment. Hypothesis → Prototype → Test → Learn → Improve. If something fails, I don't consider the entire idea a failure. It's just information."
