# Vaibhav Bariyar - public profile

Vaibhav Bariyar is a Computer Science and Engineering student at MIT-ADT University in Pune, India, and the founder of Solace. He is a full-stack developer and UI/UX-focused creator interested in AI/ML, product development, entrepreneurship, and photography. He is project-oriented and values practical, polished work that combines technology, design, and branding.

## Skills and tools

Vaibhav has worked with JavaScript, Node.js, Express, React, Vite, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, HTML/CSS, REST APIs, Python, Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras, NLTK, spaCy, OpenCV, librosa, Flask, FastAPI, MongoDB Atlas, Supabase, Firebase, Firestore, Cloudinary, Git/GitHub, Docker concepts, and deployments with Vercel, Netlify, and Render.

## Experience and interests

His work sits at the intersection of computer science, AI/ML, product development, UI/UX, entrepreneurship, and creative design. He has participated in Smart India Hackathon and achieved a Top 50 position among about 900 teams at his university.

## Selected projects

### MIT-Learn LMS
An MIT-ADT-connected learning-management-system project exploring Node.js, Express, MongoDB Atlas, Cloudinary, Firebase, and Supabase. Its concepts include student profiles, notes, modules, lessons, course resources, enrollments, progress tracking, authentication, and storage.

### Stress2Health AI Chatbot
An AI/ML project about analyzing the impact of stress on lifestyle diseases and providing preventive health guidance. It targets areas such as diabetes, high blood pressure, obesity, cardiovascular disease, and sleep disorders. Its stack includes Python, Flask, NLTK, spaCy, scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras, and several machine-learning models. It is informational and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

### AI Voice Detection Platform
A hackathon-oriented idea for detecting AI-generated voices across Tamil, English, Hindi, Malayalam, and Telugu, using a proposed FastAPI, React, Docker, librosa, audio-feature-extraction, and machine-learning stack.

### Indian Finance Game
A student-focused personal-finance learning game that explores budgeting, saving, SIPs, credit, and financial decisions. Technologies discussed include Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Firebase, and Firestore. The intended direction is an immersive, interactive simulation rather than a basic Q&A app.

### GoodFor
A family-focused product-scanner idea for food, skincare, and everyday products. The intended experience gives people a simple understanding of whether a product is suitable for them or their family. Vaibhav has worked on its product concept, UI, mascot, scoring system, branding, and design prompts.

### SwapStyle
A college mini-project concept focused on clothing swaps, reuse, upcycling, and sustainable fashion.

### Solace
Solace is a student-focused peer-support platform centered on emotionally safe conversations, peer listening, wellness reflection, and human connection. It is positioned as peer listening rather than professional therapy. Features discussed include listener profiles, booking, chat, video and in-person conversations, testimonials, and becoming-a-listener flows. Technologies considered include Supabase, Jitsi Meet, Razorpay, and payment alternatives. Its design direction is calm, safe, human, premium, warm, trustworthy, and minimal.

## Photography

Vaibhav is also a photographer and has built a photography portfolio website. He values visual storytelling, clean layouts, smooth animation, high-quality imagery, strong typography, and professional branding. His photography handle is @not_so_graphy.

## Design approach

Vaibhav prefers premium, minimal, modern, smooth, clean, cinematic, and responsive digital experiences with strong visual hierarchy, good spacing, subtle animations, micro-interactions, and polished branding. He often iterates until a design feels production-quality rather than like a basic college project.

## Working style and goals

He learns by building, experiments with multiple ideas, and prefers direct, actionable and realistic guidance. He uses AI tools as development and design partners for debugging, code, UI, prompts, documentation, product concepts, and marketing content. His longer-term goal is to build real products and eventually start a company.
